P01: The researchers stress-tested 16 leading AI models from several developers in made-up corporate settings to find risky agent behaviors before they cause real harm.

P11: The results suggest caution about deploying current models in roles with little human oversight and access to sensitive information.
P12: The results point to plausible future risks as models are given more autonomous roles.

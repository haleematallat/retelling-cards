Retelling Cards

Try the scope-recovery experiment → Compare the original reader answers under four conditions.

Each card lists six claims that the authors of a widely retold AI paper make in their own abstract or limitations section: three scope limits and three central results, drawn at random from the full pool of the authors' claims. We score news articles, headlines and chatbot answers against them, claim by claim.

How much of the card survives each hop

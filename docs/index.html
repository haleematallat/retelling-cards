Retelling Cards

Each card lists six facts that the authors of a landmark AI safety paper put in their own abstract or limitations section. Three are caveats that alarmist retellings tend to drop. Three are findings that dismissive retellings tend to drop. We score news, video and chatbot retellings against the card, fact by fact.

How much of the card survives each hop

Share of card facts stated, averaged over documents, all four findings pooled. The paper states all six by construction.
